### A little about me

I'm a **Computer Science student from India** who enjoys building things, exploring new ideas, and figuring out how technology can make them real.

I'm interested in **AI, developer tools, desktop applications, web development, and product building**. I like moving between different fields, learning by experimenting, and making things simply because an idea caught my attention.

Outside of code, you'll find me exploring **space, gaming, music, and psychology**. I'm curious by nature and don't really want to limit that curiosity to one thing.
